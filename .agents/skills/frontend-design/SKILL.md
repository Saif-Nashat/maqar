Reuse existing tokens, classes, icons, and interaction patterns. Explain meaningful design choices briefly; do not replace the brand, fonts, or component architecture merely to make a screen distinctive.

Implement the requested flow and its loading, empty, error, and success states. Keep copy aligned with actual booking behavior and the simulated payment provider. Add translation keys to both dictionaries. Check English LTR and Arabic RTL, light and dark themes, narrow and wide layouts, keyboard access, labels, contrast, focus, and reduced motion.
